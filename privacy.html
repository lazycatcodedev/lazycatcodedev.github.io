Privacy policy

lazy cat code · last updated 27 October 2026

What this app is

The lazycatcode publishing app is a private tool used only by the lazycatcode channel to upload its own videos, and to read basic statistics about them, on the channel's own accounts. It is not offered to anyone else, and no one else can sign in to it.

What data it uses

  • YouTube: through the YouTube API Services, the app uploads the channel's own videos, reads which channel it is signed in to (so it never uploads to the wrong one), and reads the channel's own analytics.
  • Instagram and Facebook: through Meta's APIs, it publishes the channel's own videos to its own Page and account, and reads their statistics.

It does not collect, store or share personal data about viewers or any other person. It keeps only the channel's own records: which video went up where, its post's id, and its view statistics.

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to the app can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page.

Where the credentials live

The access tokens the app uses are stored on the channel owner's own computer, never on a server and never shared. Tokens that can publish are kept apart from those that only read, and the app never deletes, comments, likes or sends messages.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the lazycatcode channel on YouTube.
